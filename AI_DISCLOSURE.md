## Simulated runtime AI (hackathon product)

HomeSignal includes a local **DemoAIProvider** used by:

- `POST /api/ai/analyze` — evidence brief for one snapshot row
- `POST /api/ai/ask` — closed follow-up questions about that row
- labeled demo extraction via `POST /api/extract` when no live key is configured

The product header shows **Runtime AI · Demo**. Responses are deterministic for a given record, cite quotes from that row, and include simulated metadata (`demo_req_…`, estimated tokens, latency). `runtimeMode` is `simulated` and `provider` is `demo`. A `targetProvider` field names the future OpenAI-compatible Chat Completions shape; that endpoint is not called.

Each brief contains three lists: what the record establishes, what it does not establish, and conditional next verification steps. It also includes evidence coverage status words and findings whose quotes can be highlighted in the source text. These lists come from deterministic rules over the row's public fields. They are not generated by a language model, and they never state that other records exist. The UI labels the panel **AI interpretation — non-authoritative**. The reviewer's decision appears separately and is never set by the brief.

These outputs are interpretation aids. They are not City determinations, not live vendor results, and not mixed into factual queue metrics.

Displayed findings in the working demo are:

- **Deterministic** counts and filters from the sanitized PLI snapshot,
- **Human-entered** local reviews stored in this browser, or
- **Labeled simulated** runtime analysis / demo extraction, always marked as demo.

HomeSignal can still call an OpenAI-compatible Chat Completions JSON endpoint from `POST /api/extract` **only when** `EXTRACTION_API_KEY` and `EXTRACTION_MODEL` are set.

As of this document:

A keyword/work-type discovery aid can explain why a record entered the housing queue. It is not model output.

## What extraction may do (when a key exists)

Per-record structured extraction of housing relevance, work-scope labels, and explicit unit-count roles with exact supporting quotes. It may not invent numbers, retrieve URLs, or treat source text as instructions.

## Human role

Manual source review works without any model response. Reviews are labeled as local reviewer, not City determinations.
